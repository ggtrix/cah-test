01About the hub

SureShield, Inc. (“SureShield”) provides the Critical Access Hospital Cyber Risk Reports Hub (the “Hub”) to you (the “Authorized User,” “you”) subject to these terms. If you use the Hub on behalf of a hospital, health system or other organization, you represent that you are authorized to bind it, and “you” includes that organization. “We,” “us” and “our” include SureShield, Inc. and its affiliates, and the program is delivered with BorderHawk, LLC.

The Hub presents externally observable cybersecurity signals for a specific critical access hospital or health-system domain: an interactive report, a printable report, a briefing deck, recommended actions and related services. Access is granted only after our team verifies that you work for the hospital concerned, and each sign-in requires a one-time code sent to your approved work email.

02Nature and sources of data

All data in the Hub is derived from publicly observable, passively collected sources. No system owned, operated or controlled by an assessed hospital was logged into, exploited or otherwise interacted with in an intrusive manner, and no protected health information was accessed or sought. Sources include:

  • public vulnerability records from the National Vulnerability Database and the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog;
  • service and banner information for public-facing hosts, gathered without authentication;
  • DNS records, including SPF and DMARC email-authentication records, and public certificate data;
  • HTTP and HTTPS response headers and other web-layer indicators visible from the internet;
  • Exploit Prediction Scoring System (EPSS) probabilities published by FIRST.org; and
  • public provider data, including CMS Provider of Services records, used to attribute domains to hospitals.

SureShield does not conduct penetration testing, red-team exercises, social engineering, phishing simulations or physical security assessments in connection with the Hub.

03Not a security audit or HIPAA risk analysis

Scores, bands, pillar scores, asset inventories and remediation guidance in the Hub are external risk indicators covering part of the externally observable attack surface. They do not represent:

  • an assessment of internal networks, clinical systems, medical devices, access controls, endpoints, staff practices, physical security or governance;
  • a penetration test or a vulnerability assessment performed with authorized access;
  • the risk analysis required by the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR 164.308(a)(1)), although the findings may be useful evidence for one;
  • an audit or certification under HIPAA, HITRUST, SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF or any other framework; or
  • a legal, regulatory or compliance opinion.

Engage qualified professionals before making material security, clinical-operations or procurement decisions.

04Point-in-time limitation

All data reflects publicly observable infrastructure at the time it was collected; the scan date for your hospital is shown in its report. Exposure changes, sometimes materially, as systems are fixed or reconfigured and as vulnerability catalogs are updated. We do not undertake to update the Hub in real time. Printable reports and decks are snapshots as of the date they show.

05Financial impact estimates

Breach-cost estimates and annual likelihood percentages are directional indicators only. They scale a published healthcare breach-cost baseline by your hospital's observed exposure and are not actuarial opinions, insurance underwriting figures or financial advice. The actual cost of an incident depends on factors not visible externally, including the records you hold, incident response capability, notification obligations, insurance and litigation. A modeled likelihood is not a prediction that a breach will or will not occur.

Attribution can be imprecise, for example where a domain belongs to a parent health system, is managed by a third party or sits on shared hosting. Where a hospital shares a domain with its health system, the assessment covers that shared estate. SureShield disclaims any warranty of attribution accuracy.
